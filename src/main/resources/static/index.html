How It Works: A Student's Story

Follow Rohan's journey through the four stages of the digital gatepass system, designed to replace paper slips with transparent accountability.

Chapter 1 • Application & AI Polish

Rohan Plans for a Weekend Hackathon

On Friday afternoon, Rohan needs to leave campus. He types a rough reason, but isn't sure how to sound professional. He clicks ✦ Suggest with AI, and Google Gemini instantly polishes his words into a formal request before he submits.

"No paper forms to lose. My request is logged immediately with status PENDING."
Chapter 2 • AI Risk Assessment

Gemini Analyzes the Request

The moment the request is submitted, a non-blocking asynchronous task calls the Gemini API in the background. The AI reads the destination, duration, and purpose, and intelligently assigns a safety Risk Level (LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH) along with a concise summary.

"The backend fetches AI insights in real-time, keeping the app fast and responsive."
Chapter 3 • Warden Review

Warden Sharma Reviews the Request

Warden Sharma opens his queue. Instead of just reading raw text, he is greeted by an AI Risk Badge and a smart summary for each student. This helps him instantly approve safe passes or scrutinize risky ones. He confidently clicks Approve.

"The system verifies the state and upgrades the status to APPROVED."
Chapter 4 • Gate Departure

Checking Out at the Main Gate

At 5:00 PM, Rohan arrives at the main security checkpoint. The guard checks the Gate Check-out view, sees Rohan's approved pass, and clicks Mark Exit. Rohan walks out without delay.

"Safety check: If a pass wasn't approved, the system blocks departure with a 409 Conflict."
Chapter 5 • Return & Accountability

Curfew & The Late Return Audit

When Rohan returns before curfew on Sunday, the guard clicks Mark Return. However, if any student stays off-campus past their expected curfew, the automated Late Return Audit highlights them immediately for safety followup.

"Every late return is detected algorithmically in real-time without manual paperwork."
